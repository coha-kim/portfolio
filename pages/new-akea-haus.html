Akea Haus

Akea Haus is an offline retail space that creates opportunities for people to feel connected to native plants by ‘adopting’ them as companions, so that care comes naturally.

01 — Problem

People don’t care enough about native plants to save them

Aotearoa’s native plants are highly endemic, yet occupy only a small portion of the land. While conservation campaigns have raised awareness, participation often stays short-term because the messaging can feel guilt-driven, distant or duty-based.

People care longer when care feels personal

Research suggested that people form stronger emotional attachments to plants when they carry personal meaning. That opened an opportunity to shift native plants from ‘causes to protect’ to ‘companions to care for’.

02 — Solution

Akea Haus connects neglected native plants with people seeking low-effort companionship, laying the foundation for long-term shifts in how we relate to Aotearoa’s unique flora.

Lasting impact

We focus on the long-term goal of shifting how people relate to native flora, rather than chasing immediate, small actions.

Connection over consumption

Deeper relationships with plants are prioritised over one-off, transactional interactions.

Personal connection

Through ‘adopting’, people commit to caring for a plant. This shift in mindset builds lasting associations with native plants: not just informed action, but affection and memory tied to them.

Positive reframing

We aim to replace duty- or guilt-based conservation with delightful, memorable experiences that reshape how people feel about native flora.

03 — Audience

Care begins when it feels personal.

Plants fulfilled emotional needs like responsibility, routine and ownership, with less effort than a pet. The benefits were especially popular with young professionals in their mid-20s to mid-30s who want companionship but often lack the time and finances for a pet.

Reframing native plants as ‘companions’ rather than causes could bridge the gap between knowing and caring.

04 — Offline experience

From offline to online, all in one.

A physical space where visitors are surrounded by native plants in a relaxed, social environment, building positive associations passively instead of through forced messaging.

Find the perfect pet plant

A quick interactive quiz helps visitors discover a suitable companion plant based on their lifestyle and preferences, lowering decision pressure and making the experience playful rather than transactional.

From adoption moment to everyday care

The match result is printed as a physical label with an NFC tag and attached to the newly adopted plant. It bridges offline and online, leading to a personalised plant profile and care space.

05 — Digital experience

From maintenance to meaning-making.

The digital experience shifts the focus from ‘keeping the plant alive’ to ‘building a relationship’. Each plant has a personalised profile with locally tailored information and light documentation features, creating a living record of the relationship.

Personalised profile

A personalised home page with the plant’s name encourages an emotional bond between owner and plant.

Digestible information

Quick, practical tips sit alongside deeper guidance, matching the audience’s preference for bite-sized information so people learn at their own pace.

Watering tracker

An interactive tracker uses local climate data to suggest watering, easing the ‘am I doing this right?’ anxiety while quietly teaching environmental context.

06 — Process

Testing and refinement.

The process focused on testing and refining both visuals and flow for people looking for easily accessible information online.
